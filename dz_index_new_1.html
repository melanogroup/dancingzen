Three ways to use the space

Shared Care

Give three hours,
get six

Give three hours of your time — tend the garden, help with maintenance or offer your craft. The space is yours for six hours in return.

From setup to pack-down, we take care of everything. Arrive, hold your gathering, and leave — we handle the rest.

A cleaning fee applies to all bookings. All hirers must hold their own public liability insurance.
